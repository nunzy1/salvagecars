Links to States Salvage Car Information

What follows are individual state links that provide a starting point for research. Most states have many links, which include the state legal code and more reader-friendly guidance for how they determine what a salvage car is, and the process you need to follow to make it roadworthy and legal to drive. State laws and processes vary, with each state determining what needs to be done to ensure the safety of the person driving the salvage car along with the other motorists sharing the roads. Of course, some cars are not repairable, and you'll see processes for non-repairable cars, too.

We will be working on expanding the information provided for each state. This is just a start. Additionally, we will eventually add an AI feature for you to compare state laws. That will come later.

We hope this start is helpful. There's a lot to learn, but it's interesting and, hopefully, fun.

As always, we're not providing legal advice — just a place where you can begin your own research.
