Purpose

This document describes the structure and organization of SalvageCars.com. It defines how the platform is organized, the types of content it provides, the relationships between pages, and the guiding principles used when adding new features.

This document is intended to evolve over time as the platform grows.

Product Overview

SalvageCars.com is a decision-support platform dedicated to helping people understand, purchase, rebuild, own, and sell salvage and rebuilt vehicles.

The platform combines educational content, official government resources, practical tools, and future interactive features to help visitors make informed decisions.

Rather than simply publishing articles, SalvageCars.com is designed as an interconnected knowledge platform where every page supports related content and helps answer the visitor's next question.

Product Goals

The primary goals of SalvageCars.com are to:

  • Become the most trusted online resource for salvage vehicles
  • Help visitors avoid expensive mistakes
  • Explain complex information in plain language
  • Organize information that is currently scattered across multiple sources
  • Reference official government resources whenever possible
  • Build tools that help users make informed decisions
  • Grow sustainably while maintaining editorial independence

Our Philosophy

  • We believe people make better decisions when they have accurate information.
  • We believe official government sources should be easy to find.
  • We believe transparency builds trust.
  • We believe technology should simplify—not complicate—the buying process.

  • Beginner guides
  • Auction education

  • State title information

Experienced Rebuilder

  • Quick reference materials
  • State regulations
  • Inspection requirements

Vehicle Owner

  • Salvage title process
  • Rebuilt title process

Automotive Businesses

Examples include repair shops, dealers, transport companies, and parts suppliers. Future versions of SalvageCars.com may provide tools and directories useful to these users.

Product Areas

The platform is organized into several primary areas.

Home

Introduces the platform and provides navigation to all major content areas.

Guides

Educational content explaining buying salvage vehicles, auctions, repairs, insurance, vehicle titles, registration, and rebuilding.

State Information

A dedicated section for every U.S. state. Each state page provides salvage terminology, rebuilt title terminology, inspection requirements, registration process, official DMV links, frequently asked questions, and related guides.

Categories

Content organized by vehicle type and buying intent. Examples: Salvage Cars, Trucks, SUVs, Motorcycles, Flood Vehicles, Theft Recovery Vehicles.

Tools

Interactive resources that assist decision making, including a Repair Cost Calculator, Auction Fee Calculator, ROI Calculator, VIN Resources, and a future AI Damage Estimator.

Legal

Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Disclaimers, and Affiliate Disclosure.

About

Mission, Vision, editorial standards, and contact information.

Page Types

Guide Page

Contains: Title, Summary, Table of Contents, Main content, Key Takeaways, Frequently Asked Questions, Related Guides, Related States, Official Sources, Last Reviewed Date.

State Page

Contains: State Overview, Terminology Table, Inspection Process, Registration Requirements, Official DMV Resources, Frequently Asked Questions, Related Guides, Last Reviewed Date.

Tool Page

Contains: Tool Description, Interactive Tool, Explanation, Assumptions and Limitations, Related Guides, Related States.

Category Page

Contains: Overview, Buying Tips, Related Guides, Related Tools, Frequently Asked Questions.

Design Principles

Every addition to SalvageCars.com should satisfy the following questions:

  • Is it useful?
  • Is it maintainable?
  • Does it make the platform stronger?

Features that do not meet these criteria will be reconsidered.

Definition of Success

Success is not measured solely by traffic or revenue. SalvageCars.com succeeds when:

  • Visitors make better decisions
  • People avoid costly mistakes
  • Information is trusted and shared
  • Official resources are easy to find
  • Visitors return because they found genuine value
  • The platform continues to grow without sacrificing quality or trust

Our goal is for SalvageCars.com to be the answer if someone asks, "Where can I learn about salvage vehicles?"
