SalvageCars.com Product Architecture

How our educational guides, State Guidance, and question-based navigation work together—and how the platform may grow over time.

Purpose

This document describes how SalvageCars.com organizes its information, connects related pages, and evaluates future features.

This document is intended to evolve over time as the platform grows.

Product Overview

SalvageCars.com is a decision-support platform dedicated to helping people understand, purchase, rebuild, own, and sell salvage and rebuilt vehicles.

The current website provides educational guides, state-specific guidance based on official sources, and question-based navigation. Additional decision-support tools and interactive features are possibilities for later development.

Rather than simply publishing articles, SalvageCars.com is designed as an interconnected knowledge platform where every page supports related content and helps answer the visitor's next question.

Product Goals

The primary goals of SalvageCars.com are to:

  • Become the most trusted online resource for salvage vehicles
  • Help visitors avoid expensive mistakes
  • Explain complex information in plain language
  • Organize information that is currently scattered across multiple sources
  • Reference official government resources whenever possible
  • Build tools that help users make informed decisions
  • Grow sustainably while maintaining editorial independence

Our Philosophy

  • We believe people make better decisions when they have accurate information.
  • We believe official government sources should be easy to find.
  • We believe transparency builds trust.
  • We believe technology should simplify—not complicate—the buying process.

Experienced Rebuilder

Automotive Businesses

Examples include repair shops, dealers, transport companies, and parts suppliers. Future versions of SalvageCars.com may provide tools and directories useful to these users.

Product Areas

The platform is organized into several primary areas.

Home

Introduces the site and connects visitors to seven common questions, the State Guidance directory, and educational guides.

Questions

Seven question-based journeys help visitors find relevant state-specific guidance about rebuilding, title brands, inspections, returning vehicles to the road, out-of-state purchases, selling or transferring, and considerations before buying.

Guides

Educational guides explaining salvage vehicles, auction and state terminology, buying considerations, and related topics. The guide library will expand over time.

State Guidance

Guidance for all 50 U.S. states and the District of Columbia, including state-specific title classifications, rebuilding and inspection requirements, official government sources, and related questions. Visitors can find each jurisdiction from the homepage State Guidance list.

Legal

Public Privacy Policy and Terms of Use pages, alongside educational disclaimers within relevant content.

Page Types

Guide Page

Educational content organized around a particular topic, with links to relevant state guidance and other resources where appropriate. Content structure varies by topic.

State Page

Jurisdiction-specific overview, terminology, applicable processes and inspections, official sources, “What you should know,” “About this guidance,” “What should I do next?”, and a “Last reviewed” date. Other sections vary by jurisdiction.

Question Journey Page

Explains a common visitor question and provides jurisdiction-specific links to the relevant sections of State Guidance pages.

Current Platform Capabilities

The website currently includes:

  • Homepage with question-based navigation and a State Guidance directory
  • Seven question journeys linking to jurisdiction-specific guidance
  • State Guidance pages for all 50 states and the District of Columbia
  • Guides index and four educational guides
  • Vision and Architecture documents
  • Privacy Policy and Terms of Use
  • Internal navigation, responsive page layouts, and page metadata

Additional guides and capabilities may be added as the site develops. Features described in the roadmap below are not currently available.

Future Roadmap

Future versions may include:

  • Additional educational guides, including rebuilding and selling salvage vehicles
  • Repair cost and auction fee calculators and other decision-support tools
  • Vehicle categories and state comparison features
  • VIN-related resources and potentially AI-assisted repair estimation
  • Auction aggregation and links to vehicle listings
  • Dealer and service-provider directories
  • A potential lead marketplace connecting visitors with relevant businesses
  • Affiliate integrations, with clear disclosures and independent editorial standards
  • Optional user accounts, saved vehicles, and personalized features
  • Industry updates and additional community resources

These are possibilities for later phases, not currently available features. Listings and referrals would not mean that SalvageCars.com directly buys, sells, or brokers vehicles.

Design Principles

Every addition to SalvageCars.com should satisfy the following questions:

  • Is it useful?
  • Is it maintainable?
  • Does it make the platform stronger?

Features that do not meet these criteria will be reconsidered.

Definition of Success

Success is not measured solely by traffic or revenue. SalvageCars.com succeeds when:

  • Visitors make better decisions
  • People avoid costly mistakes
  • Information is trusted and shared
  • Official resources are easy to find
  • Visitors return because they found genuine value
  • The platform continues to grow without sacrificing quality or trust

Our goal is for SalvageCars.com to be the answer if someone asks, "Where can I learn about salvage vehicles?"
