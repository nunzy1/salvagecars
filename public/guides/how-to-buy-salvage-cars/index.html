How to buy Salvage Cars at Auction

A beginner-friendly checklist for safer bidding and better rebuild outcomes.

1. Research the Vehicle Before Bidding

Review photos, title type, damage notes, and sale history. Estimate parts and labor before deciding your maximum bid.

2. Set a True Budget

Include auction fees, transport, taxes, storage, and repair costs. Avoid bidding based only on the listed sale price.

3. Verify State Rules

Check salvage and rebuilt title requirements in your state so you know registration and inspection steps ahead of time.

4. Plan Post-Auction Logistics

Have towing, storage, and repair options lined up in advance so you can move quickly after a successful bid.
