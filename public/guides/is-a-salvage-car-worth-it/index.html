Is Buying a Salvage Car Worth It?

It can be worth it when your total post-repair cost stays meaningfully below market value and title/inspection rules fit your plan.

  • Run a full cost model before bidding: vehicle + fees + transport + repairs + inspection.
  • Prefer vehicles with documented, repairable damage and clear parts availability.
  • Avoid deals where unknown damage could erase your margin.
