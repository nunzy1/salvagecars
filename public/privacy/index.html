Privacy

Last Updated: June 2026

Welcome to SalvageCars.com. Our goal is to create a useful, trustworthy resource for people interested in salvage vehicles, rebuildable cars, vehicle auctions, title regulations, and related topics. We value transparency and respect your privacy.

Information We Collect

At launch, SalvageCars.com does not require users to create accounts and does not intentionally collect personal information from visitors except when voluntarily provided.

In the future, we may collect information such as:

  • Email addresses for newsletters and updates
  • Names
  • ZIP codes
  • Vehicle interests
  • Information submitted through contact forms
  • Information submitted through tools and calculators offered on the site

You may choose whether to provide such information.

How We Use Information

Any information collected may be used to:

  • Respond to inquiries
  • Improve website content and functionality
  • Provide newsletters or updates you request
  • Develop new features and services
  • Understand visitor interests and site performance

We will not sell your email address to third parties.

Analytics and Tracking

At launch, SalvageCars.com does not use analytics services.

In the future, we may use analytics tools to better understand how visitors use the site. These tools may collect information such as:

  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Referring websites
  • Pages viewed
  • General geographic region
  • Anonymous usage statistics

Any future analytics usage will be governed by this Privacy Policy.

Cookies

SalvageCars.com may use cookies or similar technologies to improve functionality and user experience.

You can generally control cookies through your browser settings.

This means we may receive compensation when users click certain links or make purchases through affiliate links. Affiliate relationships do not affect our commitment to providing honest and useful information.

Data Security

Future Services
