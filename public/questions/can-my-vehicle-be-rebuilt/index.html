Can my salvage vehicle be rebuilt?

Whether a salvage vehicle can be rebuilt and returned to the road depends on its title classification, condition, and the requirements of the state where it will be titled or registered.

Some salvage vehicles are eligible for rebuilding, while others may be classified as nonrepairable or otherwise restricted from returning to road use.

Select a state to learn which vehicles may qualify for rebuilding and what restrictions apply.

Choose the state whose requirements you need to check. It may be different from the state where you live.

State requirements can change. Confirm current requirements with the relevant state agency before buying, repairing, or registering a vehicle.
